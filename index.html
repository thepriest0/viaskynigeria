BEFORE YOU TAKE FLIGHT

Frequently asked
questions.

Everything you may want to know before
we start planning your journey.

Do you guarantee visa approval?

No honest travel company can guarantee a visa. The legal authority to grant or deny an entry visa rests solely with consular officers and embassies.

What ViaSky guarantees is a thorough readiness review. We check your documents, financial disclosures, employment evidence and itinerary before submission so avoidable gaps are caught early.

Can I pay in Nigerian Naira?

Yes. You can pay for your itinerary in Nigerian Naira (NGN) by bank transfer or verified card. We also accept USD, GBP or EUR for clients using domiciliary or overseas accounts.

What happens if my flight changes?

We monitor your itinerary and help you deal with airline delays, cancellations and schedule changes. When disruption happens, we step in to review rebooking options and coordinate the parts of your trip affected by the change.

How early should I start planning?

For the UK, Schengen states, the US and Canada, begin around 8 to 12 weeks ahead. For faster-turnaround destinations such as East Africa and the UAE, 2 to 4 weeks is often enough.

Can you plan group and family trips?

Yes. We coordinate group flights, stays, billing and ground arrangements, including trips with guests departing from different cities. Tell us who is travelling and what brings everyone together.
