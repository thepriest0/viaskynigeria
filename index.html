+FAQs

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Message us on WhatsApp.

Which countries do you cover?

The United Kingdom (visit and student visas), Saudi Arabia for Umrah, Turkey, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Indonesia (Bali), China, the UAE (Dubai), South Africa, Tanzania (Zanzibar) and Morocco.

Do you guarantee visa approval?

No honest travel company can. Only the embassy decides. What we do is make sure your application is complete: we check your documents, finances and itinerary before you submit, so avoidable mistakes are caught early.

Can you help with a UK student visa?

Yes. Once you have your offer and CAS from your university, we help you prepare the application, review your financial documents and book your appointment. We can also arrange your flight and first accommodation.

Do you arrange Umrah trips?

Yes. We help with the Umrah visa, flights to Jeddah or Madinah, and hotels in Makkah and Madinah, for individuals, families and groups.

Can I pay in Naira?

Yes, by bank transfer or card. We also accept USD, GBP or EUR from domiciliary or overseas accounts.

How early should I start planning?

For the UK, 8 to 12 weeks before you travel, to leave time for the visa. For most other destinations, 3 to 6 weeks is usually enough. Book earlier for Ramadan, Easter and Christmas.

What happens if my flight changes?

Message us. If the airline delays, cancels or reschedules, we look at your rebooking options and update the rest of your trip to match.
